WeightMan privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

WeightMan is a weight tracker for Android. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

Your weights and profile stay on your phone

Everything you enter in WeightMan stays on your device:

  • your weight entries and their dates;
  • your date of birth, height and sex, if you enter them;
  • your goal weight, units, language, date format and reminder settings.

WeightMan does not send any of this to us or to anyone else. We have no server and no account system, so we never see it. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted from your device. Android's own device backup may keep a copy if you have backup turned on; that backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

Advertising

WeightMan shows a banner ad, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's advertising software in the app may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID;
  • approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • information about how you interact with ads, and basic device information (such as model and operating system version).

This data is collected and used by Google under its own policies:

  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

None of your weights, profile or goals are given to Google or any other advertiser.

Your consent choices

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or another region where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

If your date of birth in the app shows that you are under 18, ad requests are marked as coming from someone under the age of consent, so ads are not personalised.

Reminders

The weekly reminder is a notification scheduled on your device. It sends no data anywhere.

Who the app is for

WeightMan is intended for adults (18 and over). Its BMI information is a general guide and is not medical advice.
