My Recipe Book privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

My Recipe Book is a recipe app for Android. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

Your recipes stay on your phone

Everything you put in My Recipe Book stays on your device:

  • your recipes, including their ingredients, steps, tags and photos;
  • your private notes and favourites;
  • your name and profile icon;
  • your settings, such as units, oven temperature scale and language.

My Recipe Book does not send any of this to us or to anyone else. We have no server and no account system, so we never see it. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted from your device. Android's own device backup may keep a copy of your recipes and settings if you have backup turned on; that backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

Sharing, backups and imports

When you share recipes, back them up or print them, the app creates a file and hands it to the app you choose (for example email, Bluetooth or a cloud drive). It goes only where you send it. Backups include your private notes, so keep them somewhere safe.

When you import a recipe file, the app reads only that file.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera only when you choose Take photo to add a picture to a recipe, and the photo picker only when you choose Choose from gallery. Photos are stored inside the app on your device. The app doesn't look at any other photos.

Starter recipes

When you first set up the app with Add starter recipes switched on, the app may download the starter recipes file from our website (https://dstanyon-code.github.io). This request sends nothing about you: like any web request it reveals your IP address to the website's host, GitHub, under GitHub's own privacy statement. Without a connection, the app uses a copy built into the app instead.

Advertising

My Recipe Book shows a banner ad, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's advertising software in the app may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID;
  • approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • information about how you interact with ads, and basic device information (such as model and operating system version).

This data is collected and used by Google under its own policies:

  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

None of your recipes, photos, notes or profile are given to Google or any other advertiser.

Your consent choices

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or another region where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Children

My Recipe Book is a general cooking app and is not designed for children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG).
